# Privacy Policy — Colour Yahtzee

Last updated: August 2026

## Data We Collect
Colour Yahtzee does not collect any personal data.

## What's Stored on Your Device
- Your player name (stored locally)
- Game scores and history (stored locally)
- Game settings (stored locally)

All data is stored on your device only. It may optionally sync to your personal iCloud account if you have iCloud enabled — this data is private to you and is never shared with us or any third party.

## Multiplayer
When playing nearby games (Bluetooth/WiFi), your chosen player name is visible to other players in the same game session. No data is transmitted to any server.

When using Game Center (online multiplayer), your Game Center display name is used. This is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

## No Advertising
This app contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.

## No Analytics
This app contains no analytics tracking.
